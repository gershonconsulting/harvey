# Product knowledge — Gershon Consulting

## What we sell
Gershon Consulting (Delaware LLC, founded 2013 by Olivier Attia) builds a recurring US sales pipeline for international companies — without them hiring a US sales team. Method: **Promote – Network – Engage**, run mainly through LinkedIn social selling: content published for the client, targeted outreach to US buyers, distributors and investors, and replies turned into booked meetings.

## Who it is for (ICP)
- International tech companies (outside the USA and outside France) with more than USD 1M in annual sales and 1–19 employees, that need US customers.
- Current focus markets: United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands.
- Decision maker: founder / CEO / managing director.

## The pain we solve
- They want US revenue but a first US sales hire is expensive, slow to ramp, and risky before the market is proven.
- Founders run US outreach themselves at night from Europe and it stalls.
- They have tried trade shows or an agency and got activity, not meetings.

## Buying triggers (use these as the observation in email 1)
- Hiring a US / North America sales role (Head of US Sales, US Country Manager, AE US).
- Just raised a round, or graduated an accelerator, with "US expansion" in the plan.
- Site shows a US office, US pricing in USD, a US customer logo, or "now available in the US".
- Exhibiting at or attending a US trade show.

## How it works
Week 0: US market map and ICP analysis. Week 1: content and connections launch. Median first opportunity around day 7. Weeks 2–4: conversations become meetings. Month 2+: recurring US pipeline, with weekly reporting and talking points for every call.

## Proof (self-reported, use sparingly and only once a prospect engages)
13 years, 200+ clients from 20+ countries, 4,800+ opportunities delivered; 48 LinkedIn campaigns 2021–2026 with 799 meetings booked. Case study: MAbSilico — 1,390 leads, 252 meetings, 6 deals won. Named clients include ContentSquare, Wallix, Centreon, Gigamon.
